Piece icons: the three finalists

What survived the cull. Each row is the same seven pieces (queen, ant, beetle, spider, grasshopper, ladybug, mosquito) on a black tile and a white one, then repeated at covered-piece chip size and in a hand slot, which is where options go to die. Ink switches the two hue-coded sets between a per-tile pair and one tone used on both. Show guides draws each glyph's ink box and the clear-space hex and prints the measurements, which is how the padding was equalised rather than eyeballed. Everything is authored in one 100-unit box, so any of it drops into the Konva board, the canvas clusters and the DOM hand unchanged.
